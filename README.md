# UK-USA-youth-investment-analysis
An indepentdent Python analysis of investment participation among young adults in the UK and US using pandas and matplotlib.

## Research Question

How does investment participation among young adults differ between 
the UK and USA, and what can the available data tell us about those 
differences?

## Tools

- Python
- pandas
- matplotlib
- Jupyter Notebook

## Analysis

The project examines three areas:

1. Non-retirement market/direct equity participation
2. Stocks & Shares ISA participation in the UK
3. Cryptocurrency investment

The analysis uses data from the Financial Conduct Authority and the 
US Federal Reserve.

## Key Findings

- Measured non-retirement market participation was higher among US 
  18–24-year-olds than direct share ownership among the equivalent UK 
  age group, although the US measure is broader.
  
- Within the UK, investment participation was generally higher among 
  25–34-year-olds than 18–24-year-olds.
  
- The measured UK-US difference was considerably smaller for 
  cryptocurrency than for the wider market-participation comparison.
  
- Differences in definitions, age groups and reporting years limit 
  direct cross-country comparisons

  ## Data Sources

  Well-Being of U.S. Households
